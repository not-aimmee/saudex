Industry Challenges

What Cold Chain
Gets Wrong

We've mapped the friction points. Here's where most cold chain operations lose momentum and where we step in.

01

Silent Temperature Excursions

Most breaches go undetected until the destination. Our continuous IoT mesh logs and alerts in real time, catching drift before product is compromised.

02

Regulatory Documentation Gaps

Auditors demand chain of custody temperature records. We generate batch level certificates automatically, exportable in any format regulators require.

03

Seasonal Capacity Crunches

Peak harvest and holiday surges overwhelm static cold storage. Our elastic network gives you burst capacity without a long term lease commitment.

04

Multi Model Handoff Risk

Every transfer, truck to dock to warehouse to truck, is a temperature risk. Our protocols treat each handoff as a controlled event with signed verification.

05

Pharma vs. Food Compliance

Different sectors, different rules. We operate separate GDP and HACCP compliant zones so pharmaceutical and food cargo never share risk.

06

Carrier Accountability

Third party carriers can't always be trusted to maintain your specs. Our performance dashboards track carrier compliance and flag repeat offenders automatically.
