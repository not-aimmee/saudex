Industry Challenges

What FMCG
Gets Wrong

We've mapped the friction points. Here's where most fmcg operations lose momentum and where we step in.

01

Out of Stock at Peak

Promotions and seasonal spikes drain safety stock. Our demand sensing layer pre builds stock ahead of known triggers so shelves stay full when traffic is highest.

02

Distributor Performance Variance

Not all distributors execute equally. Our distributor scorecard system tracks fill rate, returns, and compliance by territory, and flags underperformers before brand equity suffers.

03

Short Shelf Life Management

FEFO (First Expired, First Out) is non-negotiable in FMCG. Our WMS enforces FEFO at pick level, reducing expiry write offs across the supply chain.

04

Modern vs. General Trade Split

Modern trade wants EDI and just in time. General trade wants daily drops and credit. We operate both channels from the same distribution spine without compromising either.

05

Counterfeit and Grey Market

Unauthorised product entering the chain damages brand and margin. Our serialisation and territory lock protocols make diversion visible and stoppable.

06

Returns and Damage Claims

Retail returns from FMCG buyers carry complex credit note workflows. Our reverse logistics desk processes claims within 48 hours and feeds quality data back to the plant.
