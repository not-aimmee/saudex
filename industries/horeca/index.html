Industry Challenges

What HoReCa
Gets Wrong

We've mapped the friction points. Here's where most horeca operations lose momentum and where we step in.

01

Last Minute Menu Changes

Chefs change menus. We keep a live substitution catalogue so your order can pivot to a comparable product without a missed delivery.

02

Vendor Fragmentation

Managing 8 different suppliers eats admin time. A single consolidated supplier relationship reduces PO workload by up to 60%.

03

Portion and Pack Size Waste

Oversized packs drive waste in kitchens. We offer HoReCa specific pack sizes calibrated to daily cover counts, not retail shelf sizes.

04

Seasonal Produce Inconsistency

Quality varies. Our sourcing team pre qualifies seasonal produce at origin, so what arrives is what was specced, not a field reject.

05

Credit and Payment Complexity

Cash flow cycles in hospitality are long. We offer flexible payment terms designed around booking cycles, not a fixed 30 day window.

06

Allergen Documentation

Every dish on your menu needs a traceable ingredient chain. Our delivery system attaches full allergen data sheets to every product line item.
